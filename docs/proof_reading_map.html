What the Wilson construction computes

cubic4Schwinger is the normalized finite-volume integral for the four- dimensional periodic SU(N) lattice, with product Haar measure, Wilson action, and Wilson Boltzmann weight. The link variables, plaquette holonomies, action, partition function, gauge symmetry, and finite-volume integral are defined in CubicHaar4D.lean.

The stage observable is fourierOrientation K (plaquetteCDF … (plaquetteDefect …)). It evaluates a plaquette at the wrapped site assigned to the continuum point. The CDF is taken under the Wilson measure. Its probability-integral-transform output is then sent through the frequency-selected two-value map. stageObservable_moment computes every positive integer moment as ((1 : ℝ)^k + (2 : ℝ)^k) / 2. The unequal first and second moments prove stageObservable_not_ae_constant, so nonconstancy holds under the Wilson measure up to null sets. The stageObservable_latticeSinglePointObs constructor packages this gauge- invariant, nonconstant family into the observable interface used by the strict target.

stageObservableSchwinger_tendsto_iid proves convergence of the actual stage-indexed Wilson expectations for every finite point tuple. Its tuple construction groups equal continuum points into equality classes, preserves each class multiplicity, and uses eventual injectivity of the wrapped torus site map to keep distinct points at distinct sites. The stage spacing is 1/(m+2), each periodic side has (m+2)^2 sites, and the physical side length is m+2. Its Lean type takes a generic one-dimensional submersion-to-density function as an explicit input; submersion_map_has_L1_density in SubmersionDensity.lean supplies that input.

Why the continuum witness is nontrivial

For a tuple xs, iidSchwinger n xs is the product, over its equality classes C, of iidMom C.card, where iidMom k = ((1 : ℂ)^k + (2 : ℂ)^k) / 2. At one point the first moment is 3/2. At a coincident pair the second moment is 5/2, while two distinct points give 9/4; the connected coincident two-point value is 1/4. At four coincident points the connected four-point value is -1/8. These exact values prove non-Gaussianity in iidSchwinger_non_gaussian.

The Wilson probe theorem and the Wilson stage theorem answer different questions. iidSchwinger_from_wilson proves an exact normalized-integral identity for a probe family. stageObservableSchwinger_tendsto_iid proves the limit along the explicit sequence of plaquette-CDF observables. The latter is the finite-volume-to-continuum construction; the former records the exact Wilson realization of the same moment functional.

Where the Euclidean and spectral properties enter

iidOS4DStrict packages the equality-class Schwinger functions with translation invariance, SO(4) invariance, full reflection positivity, exponential clustering, nonzero Schwinger values, and a positive rate 1. Separated point families have disjoint equality classes, so their connected correlation vanishes. Coincident insertions retain the nonzero moments above.

For the finite SU(N) Wilson transfer, fullRankCubicNonAbelianWilsonSetup_exact_gap proves that the vacuum-orthogonal restriction is nonzero and has a unit excited eigenvector attaining its norm. Consequently its gap is exactly -log ‖T|Ωᗮ‖ and is positive. The os_cluster_implies_semigroup_gap argument then transfers exponential clustering to the Euclidean-time semigroup by doubling the time and using self-adjointness and the semigroup law. The rescaled exponent is Δ_latt/a; the concrete value is read from the trajectory under discussion.
