Semantic decomposition

This page explains what the claims in the 27-component Clay audit mean mathematically. The formal declaration audit lists the Lean types and kernel axiom reports.

Lattice theory and scaling limit

The lattice stages are finite-volume SU(N) gauge theories with Wilson action and Haar measure. A stage index determines the lattice sizes, coupling, and spacing. The concrete trajectory sends the physical volume to infinity while the spacing tends to zero and the coupling grows. Gauge-invariant, nonconstant observables are evaluated on this sequence.

stageObservableSchwinger_tendsto_iid gives the meaning of the full-tuple limit: for every finite tuple of continuum points, the Wilson Schwinger expectation converges to iidSchwinger. The points may repeat. At stage m, the spacing is 1/(m+2) and the physical side length is m+2. Equal continuum points are grouped at their shared lattice sites, so repeated insertions retain their multiplicity in the moment.

There are two distinct Wilson results. iidSchwinger_from_wilson gives an exact finite-volume identity for a specially defined probe family. The finite-volume-to-continuum result is stageObservableSchwinger_tendsto_iid: it proves convergence of the selected plaquette-CDF observables along the explicit stage sequence for every finite tuple, including repeated points. The proof-reading map gives the constructions and declarations side by side. The theorem's type takes a generic submersion-to-density result as an explicit argument, supplied by submersion_map_has_L1_density in SubmersionDensity.lean.

Euclidean Schwinger properties

iidSchwinger assigns a complex number to each finite point tuple. Its six proved properties describe the structure expected of Euclidean correlation functions:

ClaimMathematical meaning
Translation invarianceMoving every insertion by the same vector leaves the correlation unchanged.
SO(4) invarianceRotating all insertion points leaves the correlation unchanged.
Permutation symmetryReordering insertions does not change the correlation.
Reflection positivityReflected test configurations define a nonnegative quadratic form, which supplies the physical inner product.
Exponential clusteringWidely separated groups of insertions become independent at an exponential rate.
Non-GaussianityThe connected four-point function is nonzero, so the field has interactions beyond Gaussian moments.

These claims are proved directly for iidSchwinger. The exact Wilson probe bridge transfers them to its probe family, and the full-tuple stage theorem identifies the limit of the selected Wilson observables with the same iidSchwinger functional. Their Lean declarations are listed in the formal audit.

OS reconstruction and Wightman structure

The target ClayYangMillsMassGap4DStrict contains an OS structure and a Wightman structure indexed by the same positive Δ_phys. Its fields also require non-Gaussianity of the OS family and convergence of the Wilson lattice Schwinger functions to that family. ClayYangMillsMassGap4DStrictOnOSCompletion strengthens this interface with an explicit OS continuation witness and an equality requiring the Wightman witness to be the result of buildStrictWightmanFromOS. The corresponding reduction theorem constructs that stronger target from the Balaban and continuation hypotheses. See the proof-reading map for the exact declaration locations.

Osterwalder–Schrader data turn reflection-positive Euclidean correlations into a Hilbert space with a normalized vacuum. A Wightman structure adds the Poincaré action and local fields. Covariance means that the fields transform with the spacetime symmetry; locality means spacelike-separated fields commute; vacuum cyclicity means field insertions generate a dense set of physical states.

When the OS-to-Wightman continuation hypotheses are supplied, the semigroup T(t) describes Euclidean-time evolution on the reconstructed space. Its gap bound ‖T(t)v‖ ≤ exp(−Δt)‖v‖ for vacuum-orthogonal states says that excited states decay exponentially in Euclidean time. Exponential clustering is the corresponding statement for correlations. The positive value Δ is the mass gap of that reconstructed theory.

At the kernel level, osConfigKernel_eq_completion_inner identifies the reflected Schwinger value for any two positive-time configurations with the inner product of their canonical vectors in the OS Hilbert completion. osTimeShiftConfig_eq_completion_inner identifies the matrix coefficient of the lifted Euclidean time semigroup with the kernel after shifting the first configuration in Euclidean time. buildStrictWightmanFromOS_config_inner gives the same identity using the Hilbert space field of the assembled Wightman object, while buildStrictWightmanFromOS_time_shift_kernel gives the semigroup matrix coefficient there. buildStrictWightmanFromOS_schwinger_vacuum_coefficient then identifies every positive-time Schwinger value with the vacuum matrix coefficient of its canonical configuration state in that same space. This makes the OS kernel, its Euclidean transfer evolution, and its vacuum coefficients explicit inside the reconstructed state space. buildStrictWightmanFromOS_schwinger_field_word_approx uses cyclic density to approximate each such coefficient arbitrarily closely by vacuum coefficients of finite linear combinations of Wightman field words.

Uniform RG and the mass-gap witness

concreteUniformRGTrajectory N hN supplies the generic uniform RG and rescaled-gap interface. Separately, the full-rank finite-volume Wilson transfer has an exact positive spectral gap, proved by fullRankCubicNonAbelianWilsonSetup_exact_gap. The Wilson-limit functional also has a rate-one cluster estimate, and os_cluster_implies_semigroup_gap derives the corresponding semigroup bound on its OS completion. These are distinct gap arguments carried by different objects. The declaration audit and proof-reading map identify their types and source files.

The complete target proposition is ClayYangMillsMassGap4DStrict. Its current strict theorem is a conditional reducer whose inputs are BalabanRGContinuumHypothesis and OSPoincareContinuationHypothesis. The unconditional theorem named MassGapInstances.clay_yang_mills_strict proves a separate finite-system gap on Fin 2. These types answer the common question whether the component witnesses already form an unconditional strict Clay witness: the OS, Wightman, stage-limit, and transfer-gap declarations are present, while the reducer inputs remain explicit.

The radial isingSchwinger family and concreteOS4DContinuumTheory are a separate distance-dependent Euclidean example. The source also proves the translation-invariance counterexample for that origin-anchored family. It is not the equality-class Wilson stage limit. concreteUniformRGTrajectory is a generic trajectory carrier with its own role; the Wilson limit is specified by stageObservableSchwinger_tendsto_iid in SchwingerConv.lean.
