The exact probe bridge and full-tuple stage limit are separate declarations. The first proves an exact identity for its probe family. The second proves the finite-volume-to-continuum limit for the selected stage observables, with spacing 1/(m+2) and physical side length m+2. Its explicit submersion-to-density argument is supplied by submersion_map_has_L1_density in SubmersionDensity.lean.

The strict Clay theorem has explicit RG and continuation inputs. The separate unconditional finite-system mass-gap theorem proves a GappedSpectrum (Fin 2) existence statement. The unconditional OS, Wightman, Wilson-limit, and finite-volume transfer declarations listed here do not instantiate the strict Clay theorem's inputs.

The original strict proposition leaves the Wightman witness independent of the OS reconstruction. ClayYangMillsMassGap4DStrictOnOSCompletion adds the object-level reconstruction equality, and its reduction theorem retains the Balaban and continuation records as explicit inputs.

concreteOS4DContinuumTheory is a separate checked OS declaration. The translation-invariance counterexample is recorded by YangMills.concreteOS4D_fails_trans_inv; it is not the Wilson stage limit. The proof-reading map gives the roles and source locations of these distinct constructions.
